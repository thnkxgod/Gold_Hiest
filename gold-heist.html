Controls and rules
  • Phone: left thumb moves. The big FIRE button is also your aim: press to shoot at the nearest enemy you can see, or press and drag to aim and shoot that way. STONE works the same: press and drag, and the further you drag the further the stone flies (distance shown in metres); release to throw. Hold ACT to interact. Settings, Edit control layout lets you move and resize every button.
  • Keyboard: WASD move, mouse aims and shoots, hold E to interact, Q noise maker, R fake steps, hold T to throw a stone (the mouse cursor sets how far), V smoke grenade, G drop gold, B binoculars, M lay mine, 1-7 CCTV camera, F siren, H hide info panels, Esc leave.
  • Shots: Guardians have unlimited shots in the base and 50 in the jungle. Robbers have 50 in the base and unlimited in the jungle. Every player has 30 HP: a rifle bullet does 3, so about 10 hits. Stones also hurt Guardians.
  • Towers: the four side towers fire a fast double beam. The main tower fires a single 30-damage sniper shot every 2 seconds (30 seconds per turn, then 1 minute rest) and sees 4x as far.
  • Breakable walls: rifle bullets slowly chip walls and crates; sniper rounds and vehicle blasts damage them fast. A broken wall opens a path for bullets and bots.
  • Wolves: two wolves roam the jungle and bite anyone who gets close. When one dies a new one appears elsewhere. Can be turned off in Match rules.
  • Vehicles: four jeeps, two seats each (driver and gunner). They can drive into the base but cannot carry the gold out. 150 HP, then they explode and kill everyone inside.
  • Hazards: poison gas clouds hurt anyone inside. Tunnels teleport you across the map (one pair even leads into the base). Smoke grenades block line of sight.
  • Robber spawn: Robbers choose where to start anywhere in the jungle during the assignment screen.
  • Ghillie suit: one suit lies in the jungle. The first Robber to take it is invisible to the sniper while in the jungle.
  • Binoculars: exactly one Robber carries them. Looking shows the sniper, but every Guardian can see that Robber while he looks.
  • Gold: a Robber can drop it and sprint at 2.5x for 2 seconds. A Guardian can pick dropped gold up and carry it home to reset it.
  • Mines and stones: Guardians share 4 hidden mines. A stone landing near a mine sets it off.
  • Power: sabotage any generator and the base goes dark, towers and CCTV die. Power returns only when all four are repaired.
  • Flashlights: a Guardian whose beam holds a Robber for 4 seconds eliminates them.
  • Robbers win by extracting the gold before the timer runs out. Guardians win on timeout or by eliminating every Robber.
